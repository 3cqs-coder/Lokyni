# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of the original purchase date.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval.

Digital goods and downloadable software are **non-refundable** once the download link
has been accessed. Physical products must be returned unused and in their original
packaging.

To request a refund, email refunds@acme-example.com with your order number.
