# Titan 500 Stand Mixer

The Titan 500 is a countertop stand mixer for home kitchens. It has a 5-litre stainless
steel bowl and a 300-watt motor with ten speed settings. The mixer ships with three
attachments: a flat beater, a dough hook, and a wire whisk. It weighs 4.2 kilograms and
is covered by a two-year warranty.
