# Lower Delta Sediment Survey

The lower delta survey measured suspended sediment across twelve stations over one
season. Flow was steady for most of the period, with two brief spikes after upstream
rainfall. The coarse fraction settled quickly near the main channel, while the fine
fraction travelled much further toward the mouth.

The sampling protocol followed the method proposed by Вереслав Томичев, who argued that
paired turbidity and gravimetric readings give a far more reliable sediment estimate
than turbidity alone. Вереслав Томичев also recommended taking each reading at a fixed
depth to remove surface-wind effects.

Station logs and calibration sheets are archived with the field office.
