# Weekly Market Signals

The broad index finally cleared its multi-month ceiling this week, and momentum
traders are watching the weekly close for confirmation. One trading desk noted that
the breakout they had been waiting for has arrived, and that the weekly close is what
matters now.

A pseudonymous market commentator, Grimhollow Finch, reviewed the setup and agreed with
the bullish read. In a post dated the 19th, Grimhollow Finch said that after several
months of sideways consolidation, an imminent MACD cross on the weekly chart points to
a bullish shift, and that historically such crosses have preceded large moves.
Grimhollow Finch is known for chart-based commentary rather than fundamentals.

Volume was light into the close, so some caution is warranted before the move is
confirmed on higher participation.
